# Which Doctor Should I Consult?

## 1. Where Should Someone Start?

If someone has irregular periods, acne, excess facial hair, or other symptoms that concern them, they can start by consulting a general physician.

A general physician can review their symptoms and medical history, arrange appropriate initial tests, and recommend specialist care when needed.

## 2. What Does a Gynaecologist Do?

A gynaecologist is a doctor who specialises in reproductive health.

A gynaecologist may help assess:
- Irregular or absent periods.
- Unusual menstrual bleeding.
- Possible PCOS.
- Pelvic pain.
- Fertility concerns.

They can discuss appropriate examinations, tests, and treatment options based on the person's needs.

## 3. What Does an Endocrinologist Do?

An endocrinologist specialises in hormones and conditions affecting the body's endocrine system.

An endocrinologist may be involved when a person has complex hormonal concerns, insulin-related problems, or other metabolic conditions requiring specialist assessment.

Not everyone with suspected PCOS needs to see an endocrinologist.

## 4. When Might a Fertility Specialist Help?

A fertility specialist may help someone who is experiencing difficulty becoming pregnant.

PCOS can affect ovulation in some people, but having PCOS does not mean a person cannot become pregnant.

A healthcare professional can discuss individual fertility concerns and suitable next steps.

## 5. Do I Need to Visit Every Specialist?

No. Most people do not need to consult every type of specialist.

The appropriate healthcare professional depends on symptoms, medical history, test results, and available healthcare services.

Starting with a general physician or gynaecologist may be a reasonable option.

## 6. What Should I Bring to an Appointment?

It may help to prepare:
- Dates of recent periods.
- A record of symptoms and when they began.
- Information about any medicines or supplements being taken.
- Relevant medical history.
- Questions or concerns to discuss.

This information can help the healthcare professional understand the situation.

## 7. When Is Emergency Care Needed?

Do not wait for a routine appointment if you have sudden severe abdominal or pelvic pain, fainting, or very heavy bleeding with weakness or dizziness.

Seek urgent medical assessment. If you think it may be an emergency in India, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.

## 8. Important Safety Note

OvaCare provides general educational information and cannot diagnose a condition or choose the right specialist for every individual.

Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical advice. Emergency symptoms require urgent assessment rather than a routine specialist recommendation.
