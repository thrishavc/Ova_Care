# Acne and Excess Hair Growth

## 1. What Are Acne and Excess Hair Growth?

Acne causes spots, pimples, blackheads, or inflamed areas of skin. It can develop when hair follicles become blocked with oil and dead skin cells.

Some people also notice thick, dark hair growing on the face, chin, chest, abdomen, or other areas. This type of excess hair growth is called hirsutism.

Both acne and excess hair growth can have different causes.

## 2. How Can These Symptoms Be Related to PCOS?

PCOS can be associated with higher levels of androgens, a group of hormones that includes testosterone.

Higher androgen levels may contribute to:
- Acne or oily skin.
- Increased growth of coarse, dark facial or body hair.
- Thinning hair on the scalp in some people.

However, acne and excess hair growth do not automatically mean someone has PCOS. Acne is common during puberty, and excess hair growth can occur for reasons other than PCOS.

## 3. What Can Someone Do?

For acne:
- Wash the skin gently with a mild cleanser.
- Avoid harsh scrubbing and picking spots.
- Seek medical advice if acne is painful, severe, causes scars, or affects emotional well-being.

For unwanted hair:
- Personal hair-removal choices may include shaving, waxing, or other suitable methods.
- A healthcare professional can discuss possible causes and treatment options if hair growth is new, increasing, or concerning.

Different treatments suit different people. Do not start prescription medicines or hormonal treatments without appropriate medical advice.

## 4. When Should Someone Seek Medical Advice?

Consider speaking with a healthcare professional if:
- Acne is severe, persistent, painful, or leaving scars.
- Thick facial or body hair is new or increasing.
- Acne or excess hair growth occurs together with irregular periods.
- Hair growth or other symptoms are affecting confidence or emotional well-being.

Seek prompt medical assessment if excess hair growth develops rapidly or is accompanied by other sudden physical changes, such as a noticeably deeper voice.

## 5. Can These Symptoms Confirm PCOS?

No. Acne and excess hair growth alone cannot confirm PCOS.

A healthcare professional considers symptoms, medical history, examination findings, and appropriate tests. Other possible causes may need to be ruled out.

OvaCare provides educational information only and cannot diagnose PCOS.

## 6. Important Safety Note

Everyone's symptoms and experiences are different. Avoid blaming, shaming, or making assumptions about someone's appearance, weight, fertility, or health.

OvaCare does not diagnose conditions or prescribe medication. Seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical concerns.
