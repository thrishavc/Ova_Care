# What Is PCOS?

## What is PCOS?

PCOS stands for Polycystic Ovary Syndrome. It is a hormonal condition that can affect how the ovaries work. It may affect menstrual periods, skin, hair growth, and the way the body uses insulin.

PCOS is different for each person. Not everyone experiences the same symptoms.

## Common Symptoms

Some common symptoms include:

- Irregular periods or missing periods.
- Acne or oily skin.
- Increased facial or body hair.
- Thinning hair on the scalp.
- Difficulty becoming pregnant.
- Weight changes or difficulty managing weight in some people.

Having one or more of these symptoms does not automatically mean a person has PCOS.

## How Is PCOS Diagnosed?

A healthcare professional evaluates symptoms, medical history, and appropriate tests to determine whether PCOS is present.

Symptoms alone cannot confirm PCOS. Other conditions can cause similar symptoms, so a healthcare professional may need to rule them out.

## What Should Someone Do If They Suspect PCOS?

If someone has irregular periods or other persistent symptoms, they should arrange an appointment with a healthcare professional.

A doctor can assess their symptoms and explain which tests, if any, may be appropriate.

## Important Safety Note

OvaCare provides general educational information. It cannot diagnose PCOS, replace a medical consultation, or prescribe medication.

If symptoms are severe, sudden, or concerning, seek appropriate medical care.
