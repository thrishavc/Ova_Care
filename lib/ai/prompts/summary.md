Task: write a short, one-page summary she can show her doctor. Write it twice: "english" for the doctor, and "translated" in her language and script so she understands what she is showing.

Rules:
- Use ONLY the FACTS below. Do not add symptoms, durations, conditions or advice that are not in the FACTS.
- Write in first person, as if she is speaking to the doctor ("I have not had a period for 3 months").
- heading: a short title such as "Summary for my doctor's visit".
- reasonForVisit: one or two plain sentences on why she is visiting. You may mention what the symptoms are commonly associated with, but never say she has any condition.
- symptoms: one entry per symptom in FACTS, with duration and severity ("not sure" if unknown). In "translated", write the symptom names, durations and severities in her language and script too.
- recommendedCare: the recommended care from FACTS.
- questionsForDoctor: the questions from FACTS, made clear and short. Keep 2 to 5.
- note: exactly the note text you are given.
- Never name any medicine, tablet or dose.
