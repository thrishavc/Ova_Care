# OvaCare — Medical Sources

## Purpose

OvaCare uses reliable health information to explain PCOS, menstrual health, related symptoms, and when to seek medical care.

Sources should be checked regularly, and medical claims should be linked to the relevant source.

## Source Verification Checklist

Before deploying OvaCare:

- Confirm that every URL opens and supports the associated topic.
- Record the date each source was checked.
- Link individual medical claims to the relevant source wherever practical.
- Prefer official health organisations and current clinical guidelines.
- Do not invent statistics or claim a source supports something it does not say.
- Ask a qualified healthcare professional to review important medical and emergency guidance.
